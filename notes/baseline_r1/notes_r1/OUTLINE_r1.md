## 1 Introduction
1. Post-hoc attribution is the method applied to trained models of industrial and degrading systems; Shapley-based temporal attribution measures a cell's importance by substituting a background value for it. [moosavi2024explainable, bento2021timeshap, lundberg2017unified, covert2021explaining]
2. The background is left to the user: TimeSHAP names zeros or the feature average; temporal Shapley works call background selection understudied and measure its effect. Since Shapley values sum to v(N) − v(∅), the background also fixes what is explained. [bento2021timeshap, nguyen2025tshap, kim2026groupsegment, papadeas2026empirical, sundararajan2020many, merrick2020explanation, mamalakis2023carefully]
3. Under mean-nonstationarity an unconditional background is far from the observation wherever the mean component is far from it; the masked windows leave the data support, where the model is unconstrained; the attribution then mixes information loss with the model's off-support response. Thesis (displayed). [frye2021shapley, hooker2021unrestricted, tonekaboni2020went]
4. Why detrending does not remove the problem (post-hoc explains the deployed model; the mean component carries the target). No citation needed (argument).
5. What exists and why it does not settle the question: on-manifold remedies for static data and images, realistic perturbations for time series, none conditions on the structure mean-nonstationarity provides; DegradX supplies ground truth under mean-nonstationarity, but its results show that scores against ϕ* on trained models cannot rank backgrounds (method gap ≤ 0.073 vs ensemble range ≥ 0.099; average event moves rank agreement by ≤ 0.04 and reorders methods in HUST and FORM). [frye2021shapley, aas2021explaining, jain2022missingness, liu2024explaining, DX]
6. This work: characterize, propose TAB, evaluate against the exact attribution of a known function per background; RQ1–RQ3 (subsection kept); case study sentence. [DX, ACIIDS]
7. Contributions (4 items, brief §2.2). Scope sentence: mechanism general, experiments on the monotone instance; battery RUL is the flagship application. Code release sentence. [severson2019data, ma2022real, cui2024data]

Missing: separating, per background, the distortion from what it explains, on models whose function is known; and backgrounds that condition on the state mean-nonstationarity supplies.
